# Lore book drafts

Drafts of in-game lore books, written from the story in `docs/DESIGN.md`. Nothing here is loaded by the game yet. Edit them freely. Once they're final, they become books in `mods/mesecraft_guides` that players find in the matching layer of the world.

Each draft notes who wrote it in the story, where it's found and which part of the story it tells. Every book reveals a little and leaves questions open, so players piece the story together across the layers.

| Draft | Found | Tells |
|---|---|---|
| `falling_star.txt` | Surface: ruins, settlement libraries | The Mese impact and the extinction |
| `miners_letter.txt` | Underworld and deep caverns | Humans enslaved to mine Mese by "gods" |
| `under_the_mask.txt` | Deep caverns, dungeons | A slave sees what the gods really are |
| `observation_log.txt` | The Moon (Grey bases) | The Greys watching Earth |
| `syntek_memo.txt` | Ruins, Mars | SynTek and the start of "The Many" |
| `voice_below.txt` | The Nether | The Hell reptilians and their cyborgs |
