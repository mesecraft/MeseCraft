Title: A Letter Never Sent
Author: Tomas, a miner
Found: the Underworld and the deep caverns

Mara,

If this reaches you, burn it after you read it. They read everything.

We are deeper than they promised. The overseers say the Mese seams below are richer than anything on the surface, and the gods want all of it. We dig in shifts that never end. When a man falls, they do not let us carry him up.

You remember the temple men who came to the village, in their fine robes, saying the gods would bless the families of those who served below? There are no blessings here. There are only quotas.

The gods themselves came down to inspect the work today. We had to kneel with our faces to the stone. I looked up. I should not have. For a moment, in the light of the Mese, the god's face did not look like a face. It looked like it was wearing one.

I am frightened, Mara. Not of the dark. Of them.

Kiss the children. Tell them their father is an honest man doing honest work. Let them believe it a little longer.

Tomas
