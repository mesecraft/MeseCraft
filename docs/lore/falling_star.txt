Title: The Falling Star
Author: an unknown chronicler
Found: surface ruins and settlement libraries

My grandmother told it to me, and her grandmother told it to her, so I write it down before it is lost.

There was a world before this one, full of creatures that no longer walk it. Then a new star appeared in the sky. Each night it grew brighter, until it was brighter than the moon, and then it was not a star at all, but a stone of fire as large as a mountain.

It fell. The sky burned for a season and then went dark for years. The forests died, and the great beasts with them. Only the small and the hidden lived: things that dug, things that swam, things that slept.

When the dust settled, there was something new in the rock. Wherever the star had broken apart, it left a yellow crystal that glows without fire and hums when you hold it close. We call it Mese. It warms the hand. It makes tools that never tire. Some say it listens.

The old folk said the star did not fall by chance. They said that others came after it, following it the way a hunter follows blood.

I do not know if that is true. But I have seen lights in the night sky that do not move like stars. And the deeper the miners dig for Mese, the fewer of them come home.
