Title: Under the Mask
Author: unsigned, scratched into a slate
Found: deep caverns and dungeons

I have found the truth, and I am writing it where they will not look.

There is a mirror in the deep hall, the one the overseers forbid us to approach. I broke a piece from it and hid it in my boot. Look into it at one of the gods, and you see them as they are.

Scales. Eyes like a snake's. Tongues that taste the air. The robes, the golden faces, the voices like thunder: all of it is a skin they wear for us.

They are not gods. They came from the sky, after the fire, when there was no one left strong enough to stop them. They have done this before, on other worlds. One of the overseers boasted of it when he thought no human could understand his language. Many worlds. Many mines. Many slaves.

They need Mese more than they need us. I think it powers their ships, their weapons, their disguises.

If you find this slate, find a mirror shard. Look at the ones who rule you. Then decide what you will do.
