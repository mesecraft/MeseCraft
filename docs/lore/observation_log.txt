Title: Observation Log 7
Author: a Grey observer, translated
Found: the Moon, in Grey bases

Cycle 4,112. Observation post, near side.

The reptilian mining fleet completed another rotation today. Their extraction of the impact crystal on the third planet has doubled since the last cycle. The native humans are now almost entirely in their service, most of them unaware of who their masters are.

The council has again chosen not to intervene openly. Our history with the reptilians has taught us that open war spreads beyond any single world. We fought them once, in the old wars, and did not stop them. We will not repeat that mistake carelessly.

Instead we watch, and we wait, and where we can, we help quietly. A dream sent to a child. A tool left where it will be found. A little knowledge, so the humans can begin to help themselves.

Note: several humans have reached orbit in crude machines. One reached the surface of this moon. It was very brave and very foolish. Recommend continued observation. Do not make contact yet.

Note: supplies of neutronium are low. The next shipment from home is late.
