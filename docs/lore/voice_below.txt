Title: The Voice Below
Author: a surviving priest
Found: the Nether

They told us Hell was a story to frighten children. It is real, and I have been there.

Far below the deepest mines, the rock gives way to fire. The heat there would kill a man in moments, but they live in it. The ones who rule the depths did not come to the surface with the others. They went down, where the richest Mese burns in the heart of the world, and there they made themselves into the devils of every old tale. Horns, fire, brimstone. They like the fear it brings. Fear makes slaves easy to keep.

They do not do the work themselves. They have servants of metal and flesh, built in the image of men but with fire for blood and machines for hearts. They do not tire, and they do not show mercy. I have heard the reptilians call them a gift from the surface, made by a company of men who trade with the depths.

I escaped with nothing but my life and this warning: whatever bargains the surface has made with the ones below, the price will be paid in us.

Pray if you still believe in anything. And do not dig too deep.
