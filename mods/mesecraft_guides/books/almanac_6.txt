This final volume of the almanac covers the crops not mentioned so far.

-- Beverage Crops --

Coffee beans and mint leaves can be farmed for delicious drinks. Both harvest well, and their drinks are filling. A hot cup of coffee even heals a little. Like the grains, mint drops its seeds and its crop separately.

At Christmas time you can also get peppermint seeds. They grow on soil, and the leaves make candies.

-- Non-Edible Crops --

The last two crops for your consideration are cactus and papyrus. I call them non-edible, but papyrus can be cooked into sugar. Its more common use is making paper, and books like this one. Papyrus must be planted near water, on regular or savanna soil.

Cactus must be placed on sand to grow. A single cactus block on sand grows slowly, so the better option is to craft a large cactus seedling. Use your cactus to make armor that pokes, and looks like pajamas!
