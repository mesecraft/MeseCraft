The world of MeseCraft is a wonderland and an endless dream. Dangers await your arrival. Poison may weaken you. Rain and storms may blow you away. You will need some knowledge to survive.

You live from the moment you appear in the world. When your health runs out, you pass away, and then you wake again. You may wonder why you don't go down to the underworld instead. That is a mystery, for now.

A day begins at dawn and ends in darkness. Morning arrives with wonders. Evening is the time for rest. Morning, then evening: that is the cycle of a day.

When dawn comes, the sun and the clouds appear, and the sun brings light to the earth. No monsters walk in daylight, but some animals are predators, and if you come too near, they will catch you. How sharp their claws and jaws are!

As soon as evening comes, night falls and darkness covers the world. The monsters wake and roam the earth, rising from the depths. Beware!

There are seas upon the earth, patrolled by fish and other sea creatures. Dive to their depths and you will discover their secrets.

The heavens, the earth and the sea, and the netherworld below: these are the realms that make up the universe. Will you find their gates? Will you uncover the secrets of the universe?

The worlds hold many mysteries, but they are beyond this book, which is about survival. Other books will reveal the mysteries, and answer the questions you find along the way.
