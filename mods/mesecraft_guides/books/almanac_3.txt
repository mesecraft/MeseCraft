The real profit in farming is finding foods that feed well and need little work. Some crops yield little and take more effort. The cash crops are the ones that yield a lot and make good food.

-- Cash Crops --

Carrots, chili peppers, corn, cucumbers, onions, potatoes and tomatoes are among the highest-yielding food crops, and they combine into great meals. As a rule, a food you cook or prepare nourishes you more than the plain crop.

My personal favorite is potato salad, made with a baked potato, a cucumber and a bowl. It is very nourishing, and with a varied diet your stomach won't tire of it.

Corn has uses besides food: it makes ethanol, a great fuel for your furnaces.

Carrots are another favorite, since gold turns them into golden carrots, one of the most filling foods there is.
