The world goes far beyond the ground beneath your feet. Here are its layers, from the highest to the deepest. Heights are the "Y" number your position shows.

-- Above --

Red asteroids: 22001 to 30912
Mars: 11000 to 22000
Asteroid belt: 3500 to 10999
The Moon: 2000 to 2300
Earth orbit: from 1000 up. There is no air in space, so bring a spacesuit.
Cloudlands: floating islands at about 400 to 600

-- The Surface --

Grassland, coniferous and deciduous forest, rainforest, savanna, desert, sandstone desert, cold desert, snowy grassland, taiga, tundra, ice sheet, and their dunes, shores, beaches, swamps and oceans. Pyramids hide in the deserts.

-- Below --

Old dwarven halls and abandoned mine tracks lie not far beneath the surface.

Caverns, level 1: about -100 to -832
Caverns, level 2: -832 to -1472
Caverns, level 3: -1472 to -2112
Sunless Sea: -2112 to -2512
Oil Sea: around -2700
Lava Sea: around -2900
The Underworld: around -3200
Primordial caverns: -3393 to -4032
Deep stone, where mithril hides: below -4100
The Nether: -17000 to -31000
Bedrock: the bottom of the world, at -30912
