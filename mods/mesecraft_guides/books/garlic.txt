Rumors of the magical weed garlic abound. This wonder plant is said to grant breath so horrible that it keeps vampires at bay. For this reason, the vampires have hidden it from us.

If we are ever to defeat the vampires of our world, garlic must be found! It grows in forests with green grass.

If you find it, begin production of this wonder plant at once. We must defeat the vampires. Wooden stakes and silver bullets won't do. Garlic is our only hope!
