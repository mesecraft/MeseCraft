Some crops just need a bit more love. I would leave these to those with a green thumb. They can be profitable, but they take more work to thrive.

-- Patience, Patience, Patience --

Grapes and green beans both yield well at harvest time. They are listed here because they need preparation before planting: place a trellis on the soil before planting grapes, and a bean pole before planting beans. You get the trellises and bean poles back when you harvest, but you must place them again before replanting.

Cabbage, pineapple, beetroot and peas have some of the lowest harvest rates of all crops. You will always harvest at least one, but the chance of getting more is low. To plant a pineapple, cut it into rings and plant the top. You don't need any tools for this.

Rhubarb yields well at harvest but needs lots of light to grow. I highly recommend extra lamps around it for quick, even growth. If you do, you'll be thankful for all the extra rhubarb pie!
