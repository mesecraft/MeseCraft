Not everything in your garden has to be vegetables or grains. There are many fruits to grow as well.

-- Fruits --

Raspberries and blueberries are both great options for the farmer, since they yield well at harvest, although they aren't very nourishing. There are two kinds of blueberries: those you plant like any other crop, and those that grow on bushes. Bush blueberries are a popular food for early settlers, since they need little work and grow back quickly.

Cocoa and apples are the two fruits that grow on trees. Apples only grow on apple trees, but you can grow cocoa simply by placing a pod on a jungle tree trunk.

Watermelons and pumpkins are the largest things in the garden, and they have the lowest harvest rate: you only ever get one. The good news is that a cutting board turns one pumpkin into four slices, and each slice can be planted. Melon slices can be planted too.

Pumpkins are most useful for early lights: a jack-o'-lantern can be switched on and off by punching it. Unlike every other crop, which harvests fastest with a sword, pumpkins are best harvested with an axe.
