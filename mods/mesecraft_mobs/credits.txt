SOUNDS
------

SnowSong by Alecia Shepherd (Sn0wShepherd), CC BY-SA 4.0. Most animal and monster sounds
  (bat, boomer, chicken, cow, crocodile, demon eye, facehugger, ocelot, pig, polar bear,
  rabbit, shark, skeleton, spider, wolf, the Halloween witch, and the arrow sounds)
  carry her name in their file tags.
  The SnowSong sound pack for the rest of the game is mods/MODULES/snowsong.
